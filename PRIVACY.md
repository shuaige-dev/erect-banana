# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 2026**

Erect Banana does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.

## How your data is handled

When you use the plugin, the image data from your selection or canvas is sent **directly from your computer** to the AI provider you configure:

- Google AI Studio
- Google Vertex AI
- OpenAI
- OpenRouter

This communication uses **your own API key** and is governed by that provider's own privacy policy and terms of service. The developer of Erect Banana has no access to this data and does not log, store, or process it in any way.

## Local processing

The plugin uses a local helper application (eb_bridge) running on your own machine to prepare images and communicate with your chosen AI provider. Temporary files created during this process are stored locally and automatically deleted after each operation. Nothing is uploaded to any server controlled by the developer.

## Analytics and tracking

This plugin does not include any analytics, tracking, or telemetry.
